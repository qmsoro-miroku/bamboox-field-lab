SUGGESTED BAMBOO USES

What can this bamboo be used for?

Suggested uses follow the predicted growth stage. Confirm the species, recorded culm age and physical condition before choosing a use. Exact age in years is not measured by this scan.

How to use these recommendations

These are general material-use ideas, not a harvest approval or a strength assessment. Requirements vary by species and product. Record when each culm emerges to track its actual age.

References: UNIDO material selection manual, PROSEA harvesting guidance, and INBAR material-use examples. These references do not validate the model's stage predictions.
