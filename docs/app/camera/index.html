Keep the bamboo stem close, sharp, and well lit. If testing a photo, upload the original in Image Scanner; filming a phone screen adds blur and glare. No box means no detection above 25% confidence.

Live speed depends on your device. Capturing a frame or recording a clip pauses live detection. Try Dataset Sample uses a public validation photo, not your webcam.

Both AI modes are available: species recognition and dataset-defined growth-stage object detection. These predictions do not certify biological age, bamboo quality, or structural safety. Growth-stage detections are not yet saved to browser journal.
